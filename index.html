FAQ

The widget shows "No words selected".
Open the app and enable at least one HSK level or category in Settings.

How often does the word change?
Widgets update roughly every 5 minutes. iOS may space updates out further to save battery.

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Everything — all 13,000+ words, sentences and fonts — is included in the app and works fully offline.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Hanzi Widget does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Period.

The app works entirely offline. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no user accounts and makes no network connections. Your settings (selected HSK levels, fonts, script preference, disabled words) are stored only on your device and are never accessible to the developer or any third party.

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to delete or request. If you have questions about this policy, contact pingshangxuezi@proton.me.
